# Privacy notice · ITC Club Hub

## Who we are
Club Hub helps ITC students find club events and register for them. Team longblackcoffee builds it as a course project at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia.

## What we collect and why
To register you, we need your student ID, name and email. We use them to identify you, stop double bookings and send you confirmations. We also record which clubs you join, so we can show them to you and check access to members-only events. When you check in at an event, we record that you came. Clubs use this to plan rooms.

If you choose, you can also give us:
- your phone number, for SMS reminders 24 hours before an event
- your faculty and year, so clubs can send you relevant workshops

## Who sees it
Event organisers see your name and a masked student ID. Club leaders see their members and attendance totals. Administrators in the student affairs office can see your details. Only our reminder sender reads your phone number. Club leaders see faculty and year as group numbers only.

## How long
We keep your details until your account is deleted. Your club membership ends when you leave the club. We delete individual check-ins at the end of each semester and keep only club totals. We delete your phone number as soon as you withdraw, or 6 months after you graduate.

## Your choices
SMS reminders, email reminders and sharing your faculty and year are all off unless you turn them on. You can change or withdraw any of them at any time in My profile. Your account works without them.
