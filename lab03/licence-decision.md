# Licence decision · ITC Club Hub
Version 1.0 · 2026-11-15 · Lead: Socheat

| Criterion                               | MIT  | Apache-2.0 | GPL-3.0 |
|-----------------------------------------|------|------------|---------|
| Anyone (incl. companies) may reuse      | Yes  | Yes        | Yes     |
| Improvements must be shared back        | No   | No         | Yes     |
| Explicit patent grant                   | No   | Yes        | Yes     |
| Compatible with GoogleTest (BSD-3)      | Yes  | Yes        | Yes     |
| Simple for future student teams         | High | Moderate   | Low     |
| Instructor / institute requirement      | None | None       | None    |

**Decision:** MIT License

**Rationale:** We selected the MIT License because it offers maximum permissiveness and simplicity for future student teams at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia who may want to fork, reuse, or build upon the ITC Club Hub codebase without complex legal restrictions or mandatory copyleft obligations. Furthermore, it is fully compatible with our testing framework (GoogleTest) and allows frictionless integration into various environments.
