# Problem statement · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 2026-10-21)

## Problem
The problem most student clubs have faced are difficulty of managements, creating announcements to reach out, and an event tracker that has made resulted to 
issues such as missed event announcements, chaotic manual sign-ups via chat groups, overcrowded rooms, and zero attendance records. 
A successful solution would centralize club information, streamline membership registration, automate event management, and provide clear attendance and room utilization records.

## Vision
For ITC students and student club organizers who struggle with disorganized communication and scattered spreadsheets, ITC Club Hub is a centralized management system that simplifies your work
like club sign-ups, event announcements, and attendance tracking.
Unlike traditional message groups, our application provides a structured, reliable, and united platform made specifically for ITC students and club organizers to use in their university life.

## Scope
- Built in C++ this semester:
  - Core business logic (club creation, member profiles, and role management).
  - Membership registration and event sign-up workflows.
  - Event scheduling and calendar management.
  - File-based data persistence (saving and loading club/user records) and command-line interface (CLI) / interactive text-based menus.
- Modelled only (web and mobile):
  - Figma wireframes and high-fidelity UI mockups for the student web dashboard.
  - Mobile app screen designs for quick event check-ins and notifications.
- Out of scope:
  - Real-time external integrations (e.g., official Telegram bot APIs or SMS push notifications).
  - Automated online payment gateways for club membership fees.
  - Integration with official Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC) student database systems.
