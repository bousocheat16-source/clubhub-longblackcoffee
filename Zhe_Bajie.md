## Working agreements TODO (checkable: a time, a number or an observable action)
1. Every pull request is reviewed by one other member within 24 hours.
2. If stuck on an issue for more than 1 hour, post the issue on the Telegram group.
3. Post a brief check-in message (What you did, what you changed, etc..) every Saturday at 6-7pm
4. No team member works on more than two tasks. 
5. Always leave a feedback/approval request on open PRs (Pull Requests) within 24 hours of being requested.
